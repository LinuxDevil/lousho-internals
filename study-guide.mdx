Work top-to-bottom through the sections below. For each, the checklist is the bar: if you can answer every question cold, you know that subsystem well enough to modify it. Each question links to the page that holds the answer; the [glossary](/glossary) defines every term; the [self-check](/self-check) is the no-peeking version.
